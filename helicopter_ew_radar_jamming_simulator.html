The 1/R⁴ vs 1/R² Power Law Principle

Radar energy propagates forward to the helicopter and then scatters back to the receiver, experiencing a two-way spherical loss of 1/R⁴. In contrast, the helicopter's Self-Protection Jammer (10 W to 100 W) emits directly into the radar receiver over a one-way path, decaying at 1/R².

Self-Protection Jamming Rule of Thumb

With a modest onboard transmitter (10 W to 100 W), a directional antenna gain of 8–12 dBi concentrates sufficient flux along the radar line of sight to screen a helicopter at standoff ranges (>20 km). Closer in, burn-through occurs as the helicopter's skin reflection overwhelms the jammer.
